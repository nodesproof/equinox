## Say this before anyone asks — real vs. mocked

"Everything that is Equinox is live on Arbitrum Sepolia: the cached Stylus program, the Solidity control, one shared volatility engine reading the **real Chainlink ETH/USD feed**, two pools with twelve series each, a keeper cron, and a demo log with every transaction hash — including one that ran out of gas. Two things are mocked because the testnet has no equivalent: **USDG** (`MockUSDG`, 6 decimals, open `mint` that doubles as the faucet) and the **L2 sequencer uptime feed** (`MockSequencerFeed`, always up, open setter that the keeper self-heals). The live identity claim is **math parity** — both `math` contracts return byte-identical prices for identical inputs (20/20 on-chain checks, the dashboard's parity column) — not identical pool quotes: the pools share σ but carry different inventory since a put on Pool A was refilled on a later Chainlink round, so their quotes differ by ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁵ and the dashboard shows that Δ. Nothing is on mainnet, nothing is externally audited, the owner is an EOA without a timelock, and the gas advantage is 2.6–2.9× on loops — not 10×."

## One-line differentiation vs. each named neighbour

- **Lyra v1** (Optimism, 2021 — on-chain Black-Scholes AMM, `baseIV × skew` moved per trade, delta-range limits, Synthetix hedging): Equinox credits its inventory-impact mechanism and adds endogenous σ_base, an on-chain IV solver and cross-series NAV, and hard solvency without a hedger. (PRD §1)
- **Lyra v2 / Derive** (off-chain orderbook, on-chain settlement): the migration Equinox's thesis is written against — Stylus reverses the pressure that pushed pricing off-chain. (PRD §1, §2.3)
- **Premia v3** (Arbitrum; ESSVI volatility oracle — docs.premia.blue › Concepts › Oracles: "ESSVI → model derived from Deribit data (subject to change)", "Updates → every minute", "Used for → Underwriter Vault & Margin Vault"; section marked unfinished, pending Amberdata): Equinox has no IV input at all — σ is computed from Chainlink spot prints. (verified 2026-09-20)
- **Stryke / Dopex CLAMM** (Arbitrum; IV from market data fed into oracles; options on concentrated-liquidity ranges): different instrument and an external IV; Equinox is European cash-settled with reserves. (docs.stryke.xyz, verified 2026-09-20)
- **Rysk, Moby** (Arbitrum options venues; IV sourcing not yet verified — PRD §18 V13 ⬜): say "to our knowledge" only.
- **Panoptic** (perpetual options on Uniswap v3 LP positions) and **Hegic** (fixed pricing): different instruments, no Black-Scholes — not direct comparables. (PRD §1)
- **OmniCurve** (London buildathon: Gaussian-priced prediction market, all math on Stylus): closest in spirit — on-chain Gaussian math in Stylus — but a prediction market, not an options venue with reserves and settlement. (`gallery-london.md`)
- **Ward** (London: credit line on tokenized stocks, Rust/Stylus vol-aware risk engine, Robinhood Chain): lending with a keeper; Equinox sells options and never depends on a keeper for safety. (same source)
- **OmniVerse** (London: partially active AMM with zero-liquidation lending on a Rust kernel): an AMM/lending hybrid; no options, no IV. (same source)
- **Truvial** (Singapore, submitted: milestone-gated disaster-relief protocol on Stylus): one of two entries stating a Stylus component (Truvial; QARBI's statement names an Arbitrum Stylus Rust WASM engine); neither is an options venue. (`gallery-singapore` refresh 2026-09-20)
- **Vigil** (Singapore, the user's other entry): a collateral-risk layer for tokenized equity on Morpho Blue / Robinhood Chain — a different problem (market-hours gaps), different chain, different repo owner; the two are complementary (options on equities need Vigil's session regime — PRD §14 Expansion, Q26).

## Feasibility — the honest "what's next"

Working today: the whole on-chain loop (Chainlink print → EWMA σ → σ_mark → Stylus price/Greeks → reserve → buy/close → settle on the Friday grid → claim → mark-to-market NAV) on Arbitrum Sepolia, with a keeper that only accelerates. Not built: withdrawal cooldown (FR-37), strike skew (FR-16), `minListingDelta` (FR-20), `EquinoxLens`, permissionless listing, a perps hedger, any mainnet deployment, any audit, any user. First real settlement: Fri 25 Sep 2026 08:00 UTC (`docs/OPS_SEPOLIA.md`). Realistic in the grant-agreement window: `i128` Q64.64 internals (measured 3.6× cheaper per multiply), the library cached on Arbitrum One with the real USDG (verify V9 first), then BTC/USDG. Not promised: two-sided trading, orderbook, governance, a token, AI (PRD §1 out of scope, §3 non-goals).
